## The morning cell check (`keeper/morning.py`, new, v r/s/t)

**`POST /morning?low=1|0`** (manual; empty = auto: Saturday or a bad high baseline), **`GET /morning`**, scheduled at `MORNING`
08:00-12:00 once a day (`morning_last`; a missed morning runs as the first thing; a manual run counts as the day's). It is busy.

- **Run sheet:** no-lock (full CA) → **all band discoveries first, while unlocked** (band lock, no reboot: #1 = serving, #2 = the
  best neighbour with a known 5G pair from `brain.REG["nr"]`, then RSRP) → a **cell lock + probe** for #1/#2 (the cell lock keeps
  the CA; the band lock collapses it) → 5G gate → A/B → restore → upload → one Telegram.
- **5G wait:** after a lock/link, `brain.wake` traffic runs while it waits up to `MORNING_5G_WAIT_S` (90) for a 5G sample (the NSA
  leg is added ~1 min after a boot), then the 25 s probe. `_has5g(n0)` = a real `nr_cell` in the samples added since the probe
  start (the count, not a time window; the previous leg's samples never count).
- **5G gate:** only 5G legs are A/B/persisted; the no-lock is the default on a tie; a band whose #1 has no 5G contributes its #2
  as A (no B); a band with no 5G is skipped. `A` = the best 5G #1, `B` = the best 5G #2 (same band fine). The restore is the raw
  quality best 5G; the restored winner gets the modem iperf3 upload (1 stream, ~8 s).
- **Lows:** B8/B20 only on Saturday (`MORNING_LOW_SATURDAY`) or when the high baseline is bad (down < `MORNING_LOW_DOWN` 20 /
  call-ready < `MORNING_LOW_CALL` 90) or `low=1`. Each band has its own band-lock discovery (the low PCI changes: never cache).
  The low is a fallback, not daily (B8 5 MHz 15.3 vs no-lock 28; the B20 394 10 MHz did 50.8/28.9 on 11-18; the B20 112 has no 5G,
  the 394's n28 is the 212).
- **Budgets:** daily 8 reboots (~30 min with the 5G wait); with the lows 12 (~45 min). One Telegram.
- **Tests:** `test/scenarios/morning.py` (the real discovery + the 7-leg daily + the 5G gate + the A/B + the restore + the 11-leg
